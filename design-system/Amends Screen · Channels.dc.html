Decided on this screen
01Direction is the primary mark on every row of the trace: an arrow in, an arrow out. It is the one thing that makes a channel legible — you can see at a glance that the agent's question left the building and the answer came back.
02Each step shows what it did to the task, not just what was said. The reply that arrives while work is held is the interesting one: it records a message and returns the task to ready, which is two effects from one email.
03An externally requested task completes on success. There is no review step, because the person who asked is outside the workspace and cannot accept anything — the agent finishing is the whole outcome.
04A contact is not a principal. They appear in threads, they are named, and they can hold no tier, own no task and take no turn — which is why they get a solid avatar ring where agents get a dashed one.
05Pausing stops intake and leaves outbound alive, so an agent mid-question can still deliver it. Closing the underlying connection is the blunt instrument and lives on Connections, not here.
