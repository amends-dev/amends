Turn 5 · black and white

3a and 4d in one colour

Without persimmon, the patch needs another way to separate from the base. Two ways for each: knockout keeps the whole mark solid and cuts a thin gap around the patch, with the stitches in the gap colour. Swap fills the patch with the opposite colour, and each stitch changes colour as it crosses the join.
