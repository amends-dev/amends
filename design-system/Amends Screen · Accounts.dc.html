Decided on this screen
01A credential is never displayed, and the screen says so rather than showing a masked field that implies it could be revealed. What is visible is when it was written and when it last changed.
02Rotate and revoke sit side by side because the difference matters and is easy to get wrong: rotating replaces the secret and every connection keeps working; revoking closes all of them at once.
03The provider list is the catalog, searched rather than scrolled — 213 entries is a search field, not a dropdown. A provider absent from the catalog cannot be connected, and the empty result says that instead of nothing.
04The form changes shape with the provider's auth: a key field for a key, a handoff button for OAuth, where the identity comes back from them rather than being typed. The descriptor appears only for providers whose strategy needs one.
05An account with no connections is a normal state, not a warning. It holds a credential and reaches nothing — which is exactly what it should do until somebody says what it is for.
