Amends design system · v2 · step two

Everything we need to build

Read against the shipped catalogue and against what the product actually does. The existing set covers conversation and task work well. It has almost nothing for the third pillar — the audit trail — which is where most of the new work sits.

keep · 11 rework · 23 new · 42

keep means it exists and only needs the new tokens. rework means it exists but changes shape under the running-stitch rules — usually losing a border or a fill. new means it does not exist in any form.

04

The audit trail

10 · all new

The largest hole. The product promises that every automated decision keeps its reasoning, and the catalogue currently has no way to show any of it. This is also where the running-stitch direction earns its keep: dense mono records with seam dividers is exactly what it is good at.

SessionLogThe agent's own detailed record, streaming while it runs. The centrepiece of the whole layer.new
SessionHeaderWhich agent, which model, which environment, how long, what it cost.new
ToolCallName, arguments, result, duration, failure. Collapsed by default, expandable to raw.new
ModelExchangeOne request and response with its token usage and cost.new
PolicyTraceWhich rule matched, which were skipped and why. Ordered, with the decision at the end.new
TriageDecisionWhere the task went and on whose authority — a rule, or an agent that was asked.new
PolicyEditorThe ordered list of conditions and actions, reorderable. Reads as a list of sentences.new
ResourceCardAn artifact an agent produced: kind, digest, size, which turn made it.new
RevisionHistoryImmutable revisions by digest, newest first, nothing ever destroyed.new
DiffViewWhat changed between two revisions. Expensive; worth confirming it is in scope.new
