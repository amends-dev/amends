Decided on this screen
01The queue shows the proposed move in the row, so you can scan what the policy wants to do without opening anything. The trace is one click away, never in your face.
02A consultation shows the travelling dash in the row and its session in the panel, because it is a real execution with a real cost — even though the task records no turn for it.
03With no policy the queue still lists everything and each row says so, rather than the screen going empty. An empty screen would suggest there is nothing to do, and there are four.
04Placing something by hand records you as the author of that decision. The trace for it is one line long, and that is the honest length.
