What the picker decides

01 Membership is per workspace One identity, several memberships, each with its own permissions. The row shows the role because it changes what the person can do once inside.
02 Counts come from the timeline “Waiting on you” is unacknowledged notifications, not unread badges. The travelling dash means a turn is running in that workspace right now.
03 Invitations are not memberships An invitation sits in its own group with the inviter named and the role it grants, because accepting is the act that creates the membership.
04 Removal is explained once A removed membership keeps its history on the workspace and disappears from this list. The state exists so nobody meets a silent permission error.
