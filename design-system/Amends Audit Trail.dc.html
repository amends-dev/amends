The audit trail

Ten components, none of which existed. The product promises that every automated decision keeps its reasoning, and this is where that promise is either kept or broken. Row grammar is taken from the twelve recorded fact types, and the routing trace shows the evidence the evaluator actually stored — expected against observed, rule by rule.

03

Triage decision

The decision is the outcome; the trace is the reasoning. Rules are evaluated in order and the first valid match wins, so the trace has to show what the skipped rules wanted and what the task actually had. A decision with no action stays current until the policy version or the conversation position moves.

04

Policy editor

reorder them

Order is the whole meaning of a policy, so each rule reads as a sentence and the number to its left is load-bearing. The sentence is generated from the conditions rather than typed, so it can never drift from what will actually be evaluated. Publishing makes an immutable version; the editor always works on a draft of the next one.
