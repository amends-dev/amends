Amends design system · v2

Tokens and base

The running-stitch direction, written down. Warm neutral paper, ink as the only fill, a dashed hairline doing every division, colour held back for status, and illustration built from the same stitch geometry as the interface.

Nothing below has landed in code yet. It replaces the blue-hued Cotton currently in foundations/color.ts and adds two files the system does not have: a stroke scale and an illustration spec. Component inventory comes next, separately.

01

Five rules everything obeys

01 Lightness separates Surfaces are told apart by paper value, never by an outline — and they do not nest. A panel inside a panel goes back to the seam.
02 The seam divides One dashed hairline is the whole divider vocabulary — section rules, row splits, dashed button edges.
03 Actions are ink The primary button is near-black. Secondary is a dashed outline. No accent-coloured buttons in the product.
04 Colour means status Inside the shell, hue only ever reports state. Persimmon lives in marketing, docs and links — never in chrome.
05 Pictures are stitches Illustration is stroke-only sashiko geometry in two colours, so a theme flip is a token swap.

07

Illustration

new · foundations/draw.ts

Stroke only, no fills, two colours per drawing: ink for the subject, line.strong for the cloth it sits on. Solid stroke means a finished thing; a 7/6 dash means a thread in progress, and it is the only element allowed to animate.

A theme flip swaps exactly two values: ink and cloth. No drawing needs a dark-mode variant.
08

The base layer

styles/global.css.ts

What every page gets before a single component mounts.

Page surface.base ground, ink.primary text, body at 15/1.6, antialiased, text-wrap: pretty on every paragraph.
Focus accept 2px ink ring, 2px offset, on :focus-visible only. Ink, not accent, so it works on every surface including the rail.
Links and selection A link is persimmon with a dashed underline — the seam again. Selection is brand.soft.
Reduced motion The travelling dash stops; nothing else in the system moves enough to matter. Transitions collapse to 0ms.
Open decisions
01Persimmon is scoped out of the product shell entirely — it survives as the workspace mark, links, marketing and docs. Say the word if you want it back on the primary button.
02Elevation drops from five levels to three. Anything currently using raised or lifted becomes flat.
03The rail keeps its own near-black in both themes, so it is a constant rather than a themed surface.
