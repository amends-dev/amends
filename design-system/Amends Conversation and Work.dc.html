Conversation and work

Rebuilt against the execution model. Task, turn and session each keep their own status and they routinely disagree — a session can finish normally because the agent successfully asked a question, while its turn is paused and its task is held. The stepper below drives the task header, thread and turn history from one stage, so those three can never disagree. The card gallery and the two log feeds further down are separate examples.
