A field is a patch stitched onto the page: no fill, a dashed running-stitch edge, and focus pulling that edge to full ink. Since a dashed pill is also the secondary button, shape does the telling apart — controls are pills, containers are rectangles.

Rest is a 1.4px dashed edge at line.seam; focus takes the same edge to ink. Nothing fills, so a form of eight fields still reads as one sheet of paper.
