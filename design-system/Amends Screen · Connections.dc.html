Decided on this screen
01Connections are grouped by purpose, not listed flat, because purpose is what decides whether one can be chosen by name. Source, tool and channel connections are referenced directly; inference ones never are.
02Model resolution is shown as a table of what each model asks for and what it lands on. Both failure modes have a row: nothing speaks the provider, or two accounts do and the tie cannot be broken.
03Scope is what the provider was asked for, and the screen says so rather than implying it is enforced here. What it actually permits is between the provider and the account.
04An account that cannot serve the chosen purpose is shown greyed with the reason, not hidden. Knowing GitHub cannot answer an inference call is worth more than a shorter list.
05Closing a connection is reversible by opening a new one; revoking its account is not. The two live on different screens for that reason, and closing names exactly what stops working.
