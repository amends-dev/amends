What each layout gives up

desktop1280 upEverything at once. Sidebar, content and the session log side by side — the only layout where you can watch a run while reading the thread.
laptop1024–1279The detail panel becomes an overlay. Content keeps its 560 minimum, so the log is a deliberate act rather than an ambient one.
tablet768–1023Navigation collapses to the 60px rail; labels move into a sheet. Every row and control grows to 44px, because a finger is assumed from here down.
phoneunder 768One column. Four destinations in a bottom bar, the rest behind the sheet, and the detail panel becomes a full-height sheet of its own.
The rail keeps its own colour Near-black in both themes, so it is a constant rather than a themed surface. It is also the only place persimmon appears in the product — the workspace mark.
Active is paper, not accent The current row lifts to a lighter wash of the rail. Counts stay mono and unfilled; only a channel needing attention earns a dot.
Agents get avatars, not glyphs A dashed ring in the sidebar is the same dashed ring in the thread, which is what stops the nav feeling like a different product.
